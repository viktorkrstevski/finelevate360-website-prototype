What changed from v1
Our own type and shapes

Noto Serif Display, Onest and IBM Plex Mono (all with Cyrillic). Square corners, hairline rules and ruled lists instead of rounded cards and gradients.

One brand, two accents

Consulting in ink and azure; the Summit in ink and green. The summit mark is recoloured from the old purple palette to Finelevate360 colours.

People, not stock

Real summit photography; speaker portraits in an ink duotone that turns to colour on hover or keyboard focus. Motion only where it adds meaning.
