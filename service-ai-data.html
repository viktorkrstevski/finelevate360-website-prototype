Regulation

The rules are now part of the design brief.

EU AI Act

AI systems that assess the creditworthiness of natural persons are high-risk. Following the Digital Omnibus on AI (political agreement, May 2026), these obligations are scheduled to apply from 2 December 2027.

DORA

AI providers and platforms fall under ICT-risk, testing and third-party requirements for financial entities.

EBA loan origination guidelines

Automated credit models must be explainable, governed and monitored through the life of the loan.

Sources: EU AI Act, Annex III; Digital Omnibus on AI (Council of the EU / European Parliament, 2026); Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA); EBA/GL/2020/06. Advisory content — not legal advice.
