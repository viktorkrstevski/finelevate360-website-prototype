Questions

Before we talk.

Do you also build and implement?

Where a build is needed, we bring in proven partners from the Finelevate360 ecosystem, select them on merit, and manage them to an agreed brief. You keep one accountable advisor throughout.

How do engagements usually start?

Most start with a short, fixed-scope strategic deep dive — or with the free AI readiness assessment. Next phases are agreed once the evidence is on the table.

How are fees set?

An expert day rate, with a volume-based discount depending on the size of the approved project. Fixed-scope phases have a fixed fee.

Which countries do you work in?

North Macedonia and the region, in six languages: English, Macedonian, Serbian, Croatian, Montenegrin and Slovenian.
