The story

“For one day, the region’s financial ecosystem was in one room.”

Banking, insurance, fintech, technology and public institutions came together to examine what progress really takes: digital identity, better payments, modern infrastructure, cyber resilience and AI that earns trust. Finelevate Digital Summit began as a conversation. The first edition made space for the connections that move those ideas into practice.

Registration, 08:30

The conversations

Three ideas. One direction.

Foundations

Trust is infrastructure.

Digital identity, interoperability and cyber resilience are the foundations of better financial services.

Experience

Make complexity disappear.

Instant payments and connected banking only matter when they make life simpler for real people.

Intelligence

Turn ambition into value.

The next chapter of data and AI in finance needs practical thinking and clear accountability.
