How we score

Transparent by design.

Each answer counts

Not started 0 · Exploring 1 · In progress 2 · Established 3. “Not sure” counts as 0 and is shown to you separately.

Six dimensions

Each dimension is scored out of 100; your overall score is the average — no dimension can hide behind another.

Five levels

Initial · Developing · Defined · Advanced · Leading (thresholds 25 / 45 / 65 / 85). Your actions come from your two lowest dimensions.
