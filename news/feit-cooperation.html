Cooperation · 5 June 2026

Finelevate and FEIT signed cooperation agreement

Finelevate and the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technologies – FEIT at UKIM electronically signed a cooperation agreement and held a working meeting with the Dean, Prof. Dr. Vladimir Atanasovski, to plan future joint activities.

At the meeting, Viktor Krstevski, Founder and CEO of Finelevate, emphasized the importance of connecting academia, industry, and the financial sector in order to develop practical knowledge, digital skills, and innovation.

As part of the cooperation, Finelevate and FEIT will work on joint events, expert lectures, workshops, research activities, presentations, and initiatives aimed at connecting students with real industry challenges.

The activities will focus on topics such as digital transformation, digital identity, trust services, data and intelligent platforms, digital channels, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, automation, and the application of modern technologies in the financial sector.

“Finelevate was created as a platform designed to accelerate digital transformation through knowledge, collaboration, and the timely recognition of change. The cooperation with FEIT is particularly important because it connects academic knowledge with the real needs of industry and creates space for students, professors, and companies to work together on topics that will shape the future,” said Krstevski.

With this partnership, Finelevate continues to build its annual program as a platform for dialogue, research, and cooperation among academia, the financial sector, technology companies, and institutions.
