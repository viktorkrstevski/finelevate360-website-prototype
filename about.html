How we work

A selected team. Never a resource pool.

Finelevate combines a focused permanent core with a carefully selected network of senior experts who have delivered complex transformation initiatives with measurable results. Clients work directly with experienced practitioners who understand strategic decision-making, technology, regulation, business analysis and the realities of implementation.

Independent

Evidence-led recommendations aligned with the client’s objectives.

Experienced

Senior practitioners with direct transformation and delivery experience.

Outcome-led

Success measured through value and results — not output volume.

Our perspective

Regional understanding. European perspective.

We combine knowledge of Southeast European markets with expertise and partnerships from the wider European ecosystem — translating international developments, regulation and proven practices into solutions grounded in each market’s operational reality.

We maintain disclosed strategic relationships with selected technology and professional-service providers. These relationships extend our knowledge and delivery capacity while preserving the independence of our advice. Our advisory work is reinforced by Finelevate research, Digital Maps, industry initiatives and the Finelevate Digital Summit.

Prof. Ezeni Brzovska, PhD

Professor of Economics with focus on product innovation

Faculty of Economics Skopje

Ezeni Brzovska is professor teaching the Marketing courses in the undergraduate and postgraduate required curriculum. Born on May 20, 1979 in Skopje.

She got her M.S. degree at the Faculty of Economics in 2006. She has published several articles and has participated in few domestic and international conferences and COST action.

Currently she serves as an Associate professor teaching Product management, Managing marketing channels and International marketing at undergraduate and Product management and International marketing at Postgraduate studies. She defended her doctoral work: “Electronic media on the telecommunication market and growth of the new media” on 12.11.2012 and got a Ph.D degree in the field of Economics. She was awarded for the best student in the Marketing Department in 2002.

In the period from 2003 to 2009 she was employed in Makedonski Telekom – Skopje in the Marketing communication department and she was in charge of developing, executing and monitoring marketing campaigns, projects and activities. She was rewarded for the best employee in Marketing and Sales Area in 2007. During this period she was part of the world known seminars, workshops and trainings, gaining extensive marketing experience.

Vasko Temelkoski

Certification expert and CEO

Intercert

Vasko Temelkoski is the Managing Director and Founder of Intercert, an independent certification body for management systems, with more than fifteen years of professional experience in the fields of standardization, certification, and quality management. He is a lead auditor for several international ISO standards and a recognized expert in quality management, information security, and risk management.

Mr. Temelkoski graduated from Ramapo College of New Jersey (USA) and holds an Executive MBA in Management and Entrepreneurship from the University of Sheffield – City College Thessaloniki, where he was a recipient of the “Boris Trajkovski” scholarship.

He has built his professional career in the United States and North Macedonia, with work experience in companies such as PricewaterhouseCoopers, Mellon Financial Corporation, and Makedonski Telekom.

In 2015, he founded Intercert, through which he has conducted audits and certifications for more than 300 private companies and public institutions in North Macedonia and internationally. He serves as Chairman of the Technical Committee for Certification Bodies at the Institute for Accreditation of the Republic of North Macedonia, is a member of several technical committees at the Institute of Standardization of the Republic of North Macedonia, and a member of the Metrology Council at the Bureau of Metrology, where he actively contributes to the development and strengthening of the national quality infrastructure.
